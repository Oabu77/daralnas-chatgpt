Our Story

DarCloud™ was born from a simple observation: businesses seeking halal, ethical digital infrastructure had nowhere to turn. Traditional cloud platforms operate on interest-based models, hidden fees, and opaque pricing structures that conflict with Islamic principles.

In 2025, we set out to change this. Starting with Dar Al-Nas's mission to provide Sharia-compliant financial services, we expanded into comprehensive cloud infrastructure. Today, DarCloud™ powers logistics, AI services, and blockchain solutions for businesses worldwide.

Our flagship platforms—OliveExpress™ for logistics, QuranChain™ for blockchain, and Fungi Mesh for infrastructure—represent the culmination of years of development. Each service is built on Islamic principles: transparency, fairness, and mutual benefit.

We're not just building technology. We're building a movement toward ethical business practices that benefit everyone, regardless of faith or background.

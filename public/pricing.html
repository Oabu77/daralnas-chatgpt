Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really Sharia-compliant?

Yes. DarCloud™ operates on transparent, fixed-fee pricing with no interest (riba), no speculative investments, and no hidden charges. All our financial operations have been reviewed by Islamic scholars.

Can I switch plans anytime?

Absolutely! You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time. There are no lock-in periods or cancellation fees.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards, debit cards, and cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDC, DAI). All transactions are processed securely with 256-bit SSL encryption.

What happens if I exceed my limits?

For the Starter plan, requests beyond 1,000/month will be throttled. We'll notify you via email and dashboard when you're approaching your limit. Professional plans have no limits.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, contact us for a full refund within 30 days of purchase.

What's included in the 99.99% uptime SLA?

Our Professional and Enterprise plans guarantee 99.99% uptime (less than 5 minutes downtime per month). If we fail to meet this, you receive service credits automatically.

How does the 30% founder royalty work?

For OliveExpress™ transactions, 30% of revenue goes to the platform founder as halal profit-sharing. This is transparently documented in every transaction on QuranChain™.

Can I get a custom quote for Enterprise?

Yes! Contact our sales team at contact@darcloud.host or click "Contact Sales" above. We'll create a custom plan based on your specific needs.
